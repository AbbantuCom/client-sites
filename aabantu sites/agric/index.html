Who We Are

Rooted in the Land,
Driven by Purpose

AgriVerde Solutions is a full-service agricultural development company committed to transforming farming across Uganda and East Africa. We bridge the gap between smallholder farmers and world-class resources.

From supplying modern farming equipment and quality inputs to running cooperative development programs and sustainability consulting, we are the partner that helps farmers thrive — season after season.

Our Commitment

Farming in Harmony
with the Earth

Climate change is Africa's greatest agricultural threat. AgriVerde Solutions builds resilience through science-backed sustainability programs that protect the land while improving harvests.

Climate-Smart Farming

Drought-resistant seeds, conservation tillage, and agroforestry to adapt to erratic rainfall patterns.

Soil Conservation

Soil testing, composting programs, and erosion control to maintain productivity for future generations.

Water Efficiency Systems

Smart irrigation and rainwater harvesting to conserve water while maximising crop output.

Renewable Energy in Agriculture

Solar-powered irrigation pumps and cold storage to cut emissions and reduce energy costs for rural farmers.
