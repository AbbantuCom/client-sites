About Kula Beverages

A story of craft, quality, and a passion for refreshing East Africa.

Our Story

Kula Beverages was established in 2009 in Kampala, Uganda, by a team of food scientists and entrepreneurs who shared a dream: to create premium beverages that celebrated African ingredients while meeting international quality standards.

The name "Kula" — meaning "to eat" or "to nourish" in Swahili — captures the brand's philosophy perfectly. We don't just make drinks; we craft experiences of nourishment, refreshment, and joy.

Starting with a single fruit juice line, Kula quickly earned a reputation for uncompromising quality and authentic flavors. Today, we operate one of Uganda's most modern beverage manufacturing facilities, producing over 2 million bottles per month across three product categories.

Our team of 350+ employees — from food scientists and quality engineers to logistics coordinators and sales teams — share a single purpose: delivering the best beverage experience, from Uganda to the world.

Our Mission

To manufacture and distribute world-class beverages that celebrate African raw materials, support local economies, and nourish communities — one bottle at a time.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most loved beverage brand by 2030 — synonymous with quality, innovation, and community impact.
