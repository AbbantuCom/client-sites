BUILDING UGANDA'S
FUTURE, ONE PROJECT

Founded in 2008, Jenga Constructions has grown to become one of Uganda's most trusted construction material suppliers and project partners. We serve contractors, developers, and individual homeowners across the country.

Our commitment to quality, on-time delivery, and expert support has earned us the trust of over 1,200 clients. We don't just sell materials — we partner with you to ensure your project succeeds.
