What We Do

OUR SERVICES

From structural steel to bespoke aluminium installations, we bring engineering expertise and craftsmanship to every project.

Structural Steel Fabrication

Heavy-duty steel structures engineered for strength, safety, and longevity — from beams to full building frameworks.

Aluminium Fabrication

Precision-cut aluminium windows, doors, partitions, and curtain walls. Lightweight, durable, and aesthetically superior.

Welding & Cutting Services

MIG, TIG, and arc welding combined with precision plasma and laser cutting for complex metalwork requirements.

Custom Metal Works

Bespoke gates, security railings, staircases, frames, and decorative metalwork — crafted to your exact specification.

Industrial Installations

End-to-end installation services for factories, warehouses, and industrial facilities — on time, on spec, every time.

Who We Are

BUILT ON PRECISION.

ForgeCraft Fabrication Ltd is a trusted provider of high-quality steel and aluminium fabrication solutions, delivering precision, durability, and excellence across every project. From conception to installation, we are committed to exceeding expectations.

Founded in Kampala, Uganda, we serve industrial, commercial, and residential clients across East Africa — backed by a skilled workforce, modern equipment, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.
