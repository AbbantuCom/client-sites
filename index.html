Why we exist

Africa's digitalisation has outpaced the laws meant to govern it.

Mobile money, digital identity, artificial intelligence, blockchain, and e-governance are reshaping economies faster than legal and regulatory frameworks can respond. That gap creates real risks — and a real opportunity.

ACLPIT exists to close it: combining rigorous legal expertise with a genuine understanding of technology, and bridging policymakers, technologists, regulators, and the communities affected by digital transformation.
