The context

01

Where law meets technological change

Africa’s rapid digitalisation is transforming public services, finance, identity systems, business and civic participation—often faster than legal and regulatory systems can respond.

This gap creates real risks, from unresolved data protection questions to weak consumer safeguards and uneven access to justice. It also creates an opportunity: to design rules and institutions that enable innovation while protecting people.

“Technology should strengthen justice, rights and inclusive development, not weaken them.”
Our founding principle
Our vision

An Africa where law and technology work together.

To advance justice, protect rights and drive inclusive development for all.

Our mission

To research, litigate, advise and convene on the legal and regulatory dimensions of technology in Africa—ensuring that digital transformation is grounded in the rule of law, respects fundamental rights and serves the public interest.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 July 2026

The African Centre for Law and Public Interest Technology (“ACLPIT”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website, contact us or engage with our digital content.

1. Information we may collect

When the website’s contact service is activated, we may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, organisation, email address, area of interest and message. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, referring page, approximate location and website interaction data through security logs or privacy-respecting analytics.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage research or partnership conversations, improve website accessibility and performance, protect the website from misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We will not sell personal information.

3. Demo contact form

This website is currently a demonstration. The contact form validates entries within your browser and does not transmit or store the information entered. This policy should be reviewed when a live form, mailing service or database is connected.

4. Cookies and third-party services

The current demo does not set ACLPIT advertising cookies. It loads selected resources from third parties, including Tailwind CSS and Lucide Icons. Tech & Legal Dialogues may later embed YouTube videos; YouTube may process device information or set cookies when a video is loaded or played. Their respective privacy policies govern that processing.

5. Sharing and international processing

We may share information with trusted service providers only where necessary to operate our website or respond to your request, and with public authorities where legally required. Some providers may process information outside your country. Where applicable, we will seek appropriate contractual and organisational safeguards.

6. Retention and security

Personal information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal, accounting and security requirements. We use proportionate technical and organisational measures, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for professional and general public audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this demo.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy as ACLPIT’s services and legal obligations develop. The effective date at the top of this notice will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to hello@aclpit.org. This is a demo contact address and should be replaced with ACLPIT’s confirmed privacy contact before launch.
